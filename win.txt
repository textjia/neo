[brussel sprouts]

Ing
. 1 lb bs
. 1–2 tbs ol
. ½ tsp st
. ¼ tsp bp
, ½ tsp gp

Opt: 1 tbs bsv or 
alittle hon

Ins
1. Ws Bs and trim 
off the bot ends.
2. Cut them in half.
3. Pat dry.
4. Toss with ol, st, 
bp, and gp.
5. Ph af to 375°F.
6. Pl bs in bas, 
cut-side down.
7. Af for 15–20 min, 
shk bas half thr.

For ext csp bs
. Coo + 2–3 min @ 
400°F.
. Don't overcrowd  
bas.
. Add bsv or Par cz 
> coo.
